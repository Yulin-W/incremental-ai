Incremental AI

An Idle Game of AI History

What is this game?

Incremental AI is an interactive educational idle game tracing the history of Artificial Intelligence. Journey across 7 Historical Epochs—from ancient automata and mechanical calculators to modern deep learning and autonomous frontier agents.

How to Play

💡
1. Think & Contemplate

Click the Think button to manually generate Insights and begin scientific discovery.

⚡
2. Build Production

Purchase historical automation (Water Clocks, Difference Engines, GPUs) for passive Insights/sec.

⏳
3. Unlock Milestones

Spend Insights to unlock paradigm shifts, boost multipliers, and advance to new Epochs.

📖
4. Explore the Codex

Select any milestone to read verified historical lore, scientific context, and academic citations.

🌌
5. Replayability & The Singularity

Can you guide humanity through all 7 Historical Epochs? Reaching the technological frontier unlocks the Paradigm Shift — a mysterious rebirth mechanism that alters the fabric of history and empowers future playthroughs. What awaits beyond the final milestone?
